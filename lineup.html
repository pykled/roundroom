How to read this
score is the composite weekly score the lineup ranks on: this week's projection × matchup × Vegas × recent form × injury. proj is the raw Sleeper weekly projection under your league's scoring (season average ÷ 17 when the weekly numbers aren't out yet). Chips explain each adjustment — hover for detail. MU = fantasy points the opponent allows to the position (this season blended with a 3-year baseline; week 1 counts 1/8, week 8+ fully). Form = last three games' actual vs projected, weighted 50/30/20, ramping in from week 3. Q / D / OUT = own injury status (0.85× / 0.5× / 0×). vs CB out etc. = a starting opponent defender at a slot that covers this position is out (+10%). Consider on a bench player = within 10% of the weakest starter he could replace. ⭐ = on your target list. "on Sleeper" = currently in your Sleeper starting lineup.
