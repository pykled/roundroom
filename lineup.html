How to read this

score — composite weekly ranking number. Your Sleeper projection adjusted by matchup difficulty, Vegas lines, recent form, injuries, rest, and weather. Higher = start him.

proj — raw Sleeper projection under your league's scoring. The score adjusts this up or down based on the factors below.

TagWhat it means
MUMatchup — how many pts this defense allows to the position on average. +% = soft D, −% = tough D. Builds confidence through the season (full weight at Week 8), and the swing is recalibrated from how much soft matchups have actually paid off so far this season.
FormRecent performance vs projection. +% = outperforming, −% = underperforming. Weighted 50/30/20 across the last 3 weeks. Kicks in at Week 3.
UsageRole change. WR/TE target share (RB carry share) over the last 2 weeks vs the season average — up 5+ pts earns up to +8%, down 5+ pts up to −4%. A snap-share drop of 15+ pp costs up to −6%. Ramps in from Week 3, full weight at Week 5.
ScriptGame script from the Vegas spread. Favoured by 7+: RB +3%, WR/TE −2% (run out the clock). Underdog by 7+: WR/TE +4%, RB −3% (must throw). Ramps in from a 3.5-pt line.
Step-upA teammate at the same position who was carrying real volume (15%+ of the team's carries/targets, or 10%+ while playing 60%+ of snaps) is Out, Doubtful or on IR. His touch share is up for grabs: 25% vacated ≈ +10%, capped at +14%. Fades once he's been out two weeks — by then the projection already reflects the new role.

How the lineup optimizer scores your players

Pocket pulls your roster and league settings straight from Sleeper, then scores every player for the week. It starts with the Sleeper projection under your league's scoring, then adjusts for matchup difficulty (fantasy points allowed to the position), the Vegas spread and likely game script, recent form, usage trends, injury designations, short Thursday-night rest, and weather. Every starting slot — including FLEX and Superflex — is filled for the highest projected total, and bench players within reach of a starter are flagged so you can make the call.

Built for your league, not a PPR default

Standard, half-PPR, full-PPR, TE-premium, superflex — every projection is run through your league's exact scoring table and roster slots, so start/sit recommendations match how your league actually scores. Projections, injuries, Vegas lines, and points-allowed data refresh daily during the NFL season. Pair it with the fantasy football trade calculator and draft assistant, which use the same league settings.
