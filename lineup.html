How to read this

score — composite weekly ranking number. Your Sleeper projection adjusted by matchup difficulty, Vegas lines, recent form, injuries, rest, and weather. Higher = start him.

proj — raw Sleeper projection under your league's scoring. The score adjusts this up or down based on the factors below.

TagWhat it means
MUMatchup — how many pts this defense allows to the position on average. +% = soft D, −% = tough D. Builds confidence through the season (full weight at Week 8).
FormRecent performance vs projection. +% = outperforming, −% = underperforming. Weighted 50/30/20 across the last 3 weeks. Kicks in at Week 3.
QQuestionable — 15% reduction applied.
DDoubtful — 50% reduction applied.
OUTOut / IR — scored as 0, move to bench.
vs CB outA starting corner, linebacker, or safety covering this position is injured — +10% boost.
TNFThursday Night Football — only 4 days' rest since Sunday. −6% applied.
WXWeather — wind over 15 mph or heavy rain at an outdoor stadium. QB/WR/TE take −6% to −18%; RBs get +4% when wind tops 20 mph.
BYENo game this week — scores 0.
ConsiderBench player within 10% of the weakest starter at his eligible slot — worth a look.
