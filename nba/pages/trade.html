      How it works. Each player is worth what he adds to your best G, G, F, F, F, UTIL lineup each remaining week: his expected best game given that week's games and injury outlook, minus the lineup without him. Players who would sit behind your starters count only as bench depth (a small share above waiver level). An empty roster spot is filled at waiver replacement level, so 2-for-1 deals count the drop or the freed spot. Playoff weeks weigh 1.5× when your Yahoo league sets them. The other team's side is scored the same way to judge whether they'd accept; without their roster, a typical mid-draft roster stands in.
