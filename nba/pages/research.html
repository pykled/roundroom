    How to Read This Page

    High Score scoring. In this league each starter’s single best game of the matchup week is the one that counts. So a player who sometimes erupts can be worth more than one who is steady but never spikes.

    Best-game value
    Our estimate of a player’s best game in a typical matchup week, in league points. This is the main number we rank by.
    Per-game score
    His average league points in a single game.
    Ceiling / Floor
    A great night (1 game in 10 beats it) and a poor night (1 game in 10 is worse).
    Form (last 5)
    His last five games compared with his season average. ▲ means he finished hot, ▼ cold.
    Boom rate
    Share of games at least 30% above his own average.
    DD / TD rate
    How often he posts a double-double (10+ in two of points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks) or a triple-double. This league adds +5 for a DD and +3 more for a TD.
    Market
    Where drafters are taking him. Value = the model ranks him higher than the market; Reach = lower.
    Back-to-back (B2B)
    Games on consecutive nights. Stars sometimes rest on one of them.
    Matchup weeks
    Most are one week long; weeks 7 and 17 last 14 days, so players get more chances at a big game.

    Scoring: points 1, rebounds 1.2, assists 1.5, steals 3, blocks 3, turnovers −1, fouls −1, technicals −1, double-double +5, triple-double +3. Last season’s numbers come from real box scores; this season’s are model estimates, not betting odds. Headshots from Sleeper.
