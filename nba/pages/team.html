      How the Power Rankings Work

      1. Strength is your best lineup, week by week. Each starter counts his best game of the week (High Score). For every remaining week we set each team's best lineup in your league's slots, using that week's games and injury news, the same way the trade calculator does.
      2. Bench and open spots count a little. Bench players get a small share for covering nights off, only when they beat the free-agent pool. An open roster spot is worth a waiver add.
      3. Bench cover is your best healthy bench player minus a waiver add. Above zero, a starter's night off is covered from your bench; below zero, the waiver wire beats your bench.
      4. Playoff weeks count 1.5× in the Rest of Season view. Switch to Next 4 or Playoffs to rank on just those weeks.
      5. Positions are slot groups. G covers PG, SG and G, F covers SF, PF and F, C is the center slot, and UTIL takes anyone, so it is ranked but never called a weakness. G, F and C are graded on depth (healthy players for their slots) and on how their starters score against the league.
