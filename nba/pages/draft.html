How the High Score draft board ranks players

In Yahoo High Score leagues only each starter's single best game of the week counts, scored with your league's own stat values. The board ranks players by the expected value of that best game over a typical week, so volatile players with big ceilings and teams with four-game weeks rise. The best-available pick weighs what each player adds to your open starter slots (PG, SG, G, SF, PF, F, C, UTIL, using Yahoo's position eligibility) against what will still be there at your next turn, and flags positions that are running dry.

About this board

How to use it during the live draft.

  1. Set your draft slot. Tap League and enter the number of teams and where you pick. With Yahoo connected, your league fills this in for you.
  2. Tap every pick. When anyone takes a player, find him (search or the position chips) and tap Draft. Tapped the wrong one? ↶ Undo takes back the last pick.
  3. Yahoo live sync or manual. Connected to Yahoo, picks fill in by themselves every few seconds (read-only: Pocket can’t draft for you). Manual board: you tap each pick, and it’s saved on this device.
  4. Watch the clock bar. It shows the round, the pick, who’s up and how many picks until yours. It turns orange when you’re on the clock. Yahoo’s draft room is the official timer.
  5. Use the top pick card. It suggests the best player for your open lineup spots, ranked by his expected best game of the week, the only game High Score counts. Tap a name for details.

Values use your league’s scoring and roster slots. Players drafted by anyone leave the board.
