How the High Score draft board ranks players

In Yahoo High Score leagues only each starter's single best game of the week counts, scored as points + rebounds + 2×assists + 3×steals + 3×blocks. The board ranks players by the expected value of that best game over a typical week, so volatile players with big ceilings and teams with four-game weeks rise. The best-available pick weighs what each player adds to your open G, F and UTIL slots against what will still be there at your next turn.
