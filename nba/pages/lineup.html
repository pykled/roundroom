How the Lineup Works

High Score in One Line

Each starter's single best game of the matchup week counts. Your week total is the sum of those best games across all nine starters.

Benching Wipes Points

If you bench a starter mid-week, every game he played so far stops counting. Put him back later and only games from then on count. That's why some moves come with a note about what you'd give up.

Expected Best Game

For each player we simulate his remaining games (injury status, back-to-backs, games left on the real schedule) and take the average of his best one, never below the score he has already banked.

Week Total and Range

The big number is your expected total with the best lineup. The bar shows the middle 80% of 2,000 simulated weeks. Win probability uses the same simulations for your opponent's best lineup.

Banked Scores

Pocket doesn't read per-game scores from your league yet, so mid-week tap Banked on a starter and type his best game so far. It is saved on this device.
