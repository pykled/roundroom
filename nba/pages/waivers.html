          How Waivers Work Here

          What the Numbers Mean

          Your weekly score counts each starter's single best game. A player helps you if his best game beats the starter he'd replace. Gain is how many points your best lineup goes up after the move, for this week and for the next few weeks (later weeks count a little less).

          Why Games Matter So Much

          More games means more chances at a big night. A player with 4 games this week often beats a better player with 2. Green chips mark 4-game weeks. Weeks 7 and 17 last 14 days, so games pile up.

          Free Agent or Waivers

          • Free agent: add him now. He can play today.
          • Waivers: you put in a claim. It clears after the waiver period, so he misses today's game.
          • Your league uses a rolling list. A claim that goes through sends you to the back of the line; one that fails keeps your spot. When a claim only adds a little, we suggest keeping your spot for a bigger pickup later.

          Add Budget

          Your league allows a set number of adds per week. Every free agent or waiver add uses one. When you're out, we stop suggesting moves.

          Drops

          We suggest dropping whoever your lineup misses least. We never suggest dropping a player who's worth more over the season than the free agent replacing him.
