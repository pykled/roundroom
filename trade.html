How scoring works
Both sides are scored on league-specific VORP (value over a replacement player for your slots and team count), blended 70/30 with FantasyCalc market consensus — the same numbers as the lineup optimizer and team page. The verdict is FAIR when the two totals are within 10%, WIN when you receive more, LOSE when you give more. Connect Sleeper to score with your league's exact settings and check the fit for your actual roster. Roster Fit scales each player by your need at that position: ▲ Critical ×1.30 · ▲ Upgrade ×1.15 · △ Thin ×1.10 · – Solid ×1.00 · ▼ Deep ×0.90 · ◆ Loaded ×0.80. A position you need makes incoming players worth more and outgoing players cost more, so a like-for-like swap nets out. Hover or tap a position chip for the full read on that spot.
